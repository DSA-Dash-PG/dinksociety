Captain fee split

A new Split with your team card on the captain portal's existing Billing tab. The captain picks Flat split or Per game, the app works out what every player owes, players pay the captain on Venmo, and the app keeps the ledger. No money moves through the app — same Venmo-first approach the league already uses.

The inputs and the paid / owes pills below are live — change a number or tap a pill.

Part 3

Decisions for you before I build

1 · Does the captain count as a player?Mockup assumes yes — the captain is one of the 10 shares and is auto-marked paid. The alternative is the captain splits the whole fee across everyone else.
2 · RoundingMockup rounds flat shares up to the whole dollar so the captain is never short; any extra dollar or two shows as “team cushion”. Alternative: exact cents.
3 · Per game: which weeks bill?Mockup bills every finalized match including rivalry / playoff weeks (168 player-games). If playoffs shouldn't bill, the suggested rate rises to cover the fee in 6 weeks.
4 · Who can see the ledger?Mockup: captain and co-captain see everyone; a player sees only their own share. The league admin sees nothing unless you want an admin view.
