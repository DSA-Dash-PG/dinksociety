Step one

Put the app on your phone

The Dink Society app is the site saved to your home screen. It takes a minute, and one sign-in covers both the player side and the Captain Portal.

  1. Open the portal

    On your phone, go to dinksociety.app and tap Player Login.

  2. Add it to your home screen

    iPhone (Safari): Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    Android (Chrome): the three-dot menu, then Add to Home screen.

  3. Sign in with the code

    Enter your email and we send a link and a 6-digit code. In the home-screen app, type the code. The link opens your browser, not the app.

    No password. You stay signed in for 30 days.

  4. Captains: tap Captain

    Captains and co-captains get a Captain switch at the top of the app. It moves you between your player view and the Captain Portal.

No email arriving? You can only sign in with the email on your team's roster. Players: ask your captain to add it. Captains: use the email you registered with, and check spam.

The routine

Your week, start to finish

The app does the chasing for you. Your job is to look at it at the right moments.

  1. 4 days before

    Availability reminders start going out

    Players who haven't answered get an email with one-tap I'm in and Can't make it buttons, once a day until they reply or the lineup locks. Subs are not auto-reminded, so use Nudge on any sub you want.

  2. Early in the week

    Check Who's in, then draft the lineup

    Players marked out drop off your lineup picker automatically. If someone texts you instead of using the app, set them In or Out yourself. Save your draft as often as you like.

  3. 10 minutes before start · hard cutoff

    Lock your lineup

    Locking emails every player their games for the night. Locked early and something changed? Unlock it, fix it, lock it again. You can do that right up to the cutoff. After that, only the league can change it.

  4. At game time

    Both lineups reveal together

    You see your opponent's lineup and they see yours at the same moment, as long as both teams have locked. Scoring opens then too.

  5. Match night

    Score it courtside, game by game

    The home captain enters each score and it saves as you type. The away captain confirms it. Each game appears on the Live page as soon as it's confirmed.

  6. Before you leave the courts

    Both captains sign off

    Once all 12 games are confirmed, tap Review & sign off, then Sign & submit. When both captains have signed, the match is final and the standings update.

  7. The morning after

    The Receipt and The Drop

    Everyone who played gets a personal recap email: their games, their rank movement, the team's night and the standings. It also asks if they're in for next week, so the cycle starts again. The Drop, our weekly editorial, covers the whole league.

Why keeping it current matters

The app is how the night actually runs. Every lineup, score, standing and email comes from what's in it, so anything out of date shows up as a real problem for your team, your opponent, or the whole league.

If your lineup isn't locked

Nothing reveals, and nobody can score

Lineups reveal and scoring opens only when both teams have locked. One missing lineup holds up the match for everyone on both sides.

If a player never answers

The app treats them as available

Silence counts as "in". Chase the quiet ones before you build a lineup around someone who isn't coming.

If a player has no email or gender on file

They're invisible, and they can block your lineup

No email means no sign-in, no lineup email and no reminders. No gender means you can't lock a lineup with them in it.

If someone isn't on your roster

They can't play

A non-rostered player is an automatic forfeit, and new players need league approval before you can pick them. Add them days ahead.

If scores aren't confirmed and signed

The night didn't happen yet

Nothing is public until the away captain confirms it. Standings and rankings don't move until both captains sign off. The morning-after recap waits for every match from the night, so one open scoresheet holds up the whole league's email.

If a waiver isn't signed

That player is locked out of the app

The waiver comes first when they sign in. Until it's signed they can't see the lineup, answer availability or use team chat.

The habit

Open the app at least three times a week: early in the week to see who's in, the day before to finish the lineup, and on game day to lock it, check for league announcements such as a rainout, and score the match. Start on Home. If Needs you is empty, you're done.

Me

Your DSR, record, week-by-week results, best partners and trophies. DSR is one score out of 100 for how you're playing. Winning counts most, and it refreshes after every match night.

  1. Get the app and sign inFollow Put the app on your phone above. Use the email your captain put on the roster.
  2. Sign the waiverIt comes up the first time you sign in. Read to the bottom and type your name. About two minutes, once per season.
  3. Say if you're in, every weekTap I'm in or Can't make it on Home, or straight from the reminder email. If you say nothing, you're treated as available.
  4. Check your lineupYou'll get an email when your captain locks it. The full sheet is under Matches, then Lineup.
  5. Use team chatThe speech bubble at the top. Only your teammates can see it.
  6. Finish your profileTap your photo, then Edit my profile: add a photo and your shirt size.

Match night in six lines

The short version. The full rules have the detail.

12 gamesTwo rounds of six. Each round: G1 women's doubles, G2 men's doubles, G3 to G6 mixed.
To 11, win by 1No win-by-two in the regular season. Switch ends when the first side reaches 6.
6 games maxPer player, per night. The same pair can only play together once per round.
4 points a nightWin a round, 2 points. Tie it 3–3, 1 point each. Match points set the standings.
Home serves firstIn Round 1, and the away team picks the side. Round 2 flips it.
30 minutes earlyBe at the venue and checked in half an hour before your start time. Rostered players only.
