Kings Court Ladder Recap

Sep 14, 2026 · Dink House · 12 players · 8 rounds · 3 courts · 24 games

The Recap

Jason P. Shows Up Once, Leaves With the Crown

Twelve men, eight rounds, and the first Dink Society ladder ever logged at the Dink House. Jason P. walked in with no ladder history at all and walked out 7-1 with a plus 34 point differential and the crown. His only blemish came in round five, when Chandler H. and Matt P. took him down 11-7. Jason answered by winning his final three, closing 11-6 with Matt as his partner, which is a very polite way to settle a score.

Ryan H. did everything except finish first. He went 6-2 with a plus 27 and posted the best night rating in the field at 84.5, and here is the cruel part: both of his losses came in games where Jason was on the other side of the net. Round four, Matt and Jason beat him 11-6. Round eight, same pair, same score. Matt took third at 6-2, separated from second by nine points of differential. Chandler finished fourth at 5-3 and tied for the most wins on Court A, the top court, with four.

Ten of the twelve players reached Court A at some point, so this was never a closed shop at the top. The very first game of the night was an 11-10 escape, Matt and Chandler over Richard H. and Guilbert B., and it set the tone. Only one other game all evening was decided by a single point.

Guilbert finished 3-5 and still walked away with the most interesting stat line on the sheet: three comeback wins, more than anyone, and MVP shares in two separate rounds including a plus 9 in the finale. Robert D. climbed a spot and took MVP honors in back-to-back rounds five and six. Yoshi N. collected three MVP nods, more than anybody, and ended the night on a win.

Erick L. split 4-4 and helped hang the biggest margin of the night, an 11-2 alongside Matt in round seven. Kevin V. climbed a spot and survived the night's other one-pointer, a 7-6 with Richard in round seven. Richard took a round-four MVP and closed on a two-game streak, the last of them an 11-2. Quan V. found his second win of the night 9-5 in round six, and Teddie L. got his 11-8 in round two with Erick. Nobody got out of the Dink House clean.

One last note for the season board: Jason banked 95 season points on a single night out, which is what a 7-1 debut buys you. Ryan added 86 and stays second on the season table behind Richard, who has now logged eighteen nights to Ryan's twelve. The gap is real. The schedule is long.
