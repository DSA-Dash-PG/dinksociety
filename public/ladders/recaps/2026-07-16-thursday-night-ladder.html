The Recap

Richard Hak Posts the Best Record of the Night Without Ever Owning the Kitchen

Richard Hak put up the best record and the best point differential of the night, going 7-2 with a +44 point differential on a 5-match win streak. He spent five of the nine rounds parked in the kitchen court — second-most tenure behind only Wes — before a late slide to Court 2 in Round 8 dropped him back down. He closed the night one rung below the top, on Court 3.

The kitchen court itself belonged to Wes, who held Court 4 for seven of the nine rounds before sliding back to Court 3 in the final round. Ryan H made the more dramatic trip: he opened the night on the bottom court and worked his way up to the kitchen by Round 5, the biggest single-night climb of anyone on the board.

Round 2 produced the loudest scoreline of the night — twice. Richard Hak and Wes blanked Kai Pylkkanen and Teddie Liu 11-0 on Court 4, and the very same scoreline showed up again in Round 6, when Yoshi Nogimura and Fiat T put up an 11-0 result against Gabe Ramos and Neslyn on Court 1.

Neslyn had a night to forget, going 0-9 with a -70 point differential — on the wrong end of that Round 6 shutout among several other lopsided losses — and never left the bottom court all night.
