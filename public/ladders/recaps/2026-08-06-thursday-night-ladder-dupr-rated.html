Thursday Night Ladder Recap

Aug 6, 2026 · South Bay Tennis & Pickleball Center · 16 players · 8 rounds · 4 courts

The Recap

Matt Pasqualetto Runs the Table While Five Rookies Get Their First DUPR Number

Matt P. ran the ladder Thursday. He finished 7-1 with a +46 point differential, the kind of line that ends the night with your name at the top, and DUPR made it official: his rating jumped from 3.152 to 3.649, a +0.497 swing that was the biggest move of the night in either direction.

Elijah H. (6-2, +39) and Ryan H. (6-2, +19) rounded out the podium, and Ryan basically never left home. He held 3A, the kitchen court, for seven of the eight rounds. His only real scare came in Round 2, a 9-9 nailbiter that went his way and Karen T.'s on the win-by-one tiebreak, first team to hit 9 gets the win.

The scoreboard and the rating sheet did not always agree. Chandler H. went a perfectly fine 4-4 and still watched his DUPR slide from 3.955 to 3.550, the steepest drop of the night. Angel M. climbed three full courts, from 3D all the way up to the kitchen, on a 4-4 night that barely moved her number at all, 2.806 to 2.807. Point differential and court position told two different stories all night.

Five players logged their first-ever rated DUPR match: Dev C., Fiat T., Karen T., Annie K., and Rick B. Debuts are not always kind. Rick opened his DUPR account at 2.571 after a 1-7 night, but he played all eight rounds to get there, and every one of the five now has a number on the board. Welcome to the ladder.
