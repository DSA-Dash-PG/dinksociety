Aloha Night Ladder Recap

Jul 14, 2026 · South Bay Tennis & Pickleball Center · 16 players · 9 rounds · 4 courts

The Recap

Richard Hak Goes 8-1 and Still Has to Share the Streak Record on Aloha Night

Aloha Night belonged to Richard Hak. He went 8-1, the best record of the night, riding a 6-match win streak while also climbing from the bottom court all the way to the kitchen — arriving by Round 6 and staying there through the finish. It's the rare night where one player owns both the standings and the court climb at once.

KC Terada matched his win streak stride for stride, also stringing together six straight wins on her way to a 7-2 night and second place overall. She and Ryan H tied for the most kitchen-court tenure on the night, six of nine rounds apiece, even though neither finished there — KC slid back to Court 2 late, and Ryan H settled on Court 3.

Round 4 brought the loudest scoreline of the night, twice over. Ryan H and Fiat T blanked Elliot and T Petitt 11-0 on Court 3, while one court down, Richard Hak and Kayo Hayashi handed Emanual Escamilla and Angel Munar the same 11-0 result.

Kayo Hayashi had the mirror image of Richard Hak's night without the record to match it — an even 5-4 finish, but a climb from the bottom court all the way to the kitchen, tied with Richard Hak for the biggest jump of the night. T Petitt had the opposite arc: she started on the kitchen court and finished on the bottom, a three-court fall to go with a 2-7 record and a -33 point differential, tied for the worst of the night.
