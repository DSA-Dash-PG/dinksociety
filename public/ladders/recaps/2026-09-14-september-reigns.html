The Dink Society

September Reigns Recap

Sep 14, 2026 · South End Racquet & Health Club, Torrance · 16 players · 9 rounds · 4 courts · 36 games

The Recap

Three Women Went 7-2. Only One Got the Crown.

Sixteen players, nine rounds, and a three-way logjam at 7-2 right at the summit. Christyn R. took the crown on point differential at plus 41, closing the night with an 11-0 on court four alongside Niko K. She also climbed three spots, the biggest move anyone made all evening. Here is the part that should keep her up at night: both of Christyn's losses were by a single point, a 5-6 in round five and an 8-9 in round six. Two more points in nine rounds and this is not even a conversation.

Candice C. came agonizingly close. She opened the night with seven straight wins, the longest run anybody managed, three of them by nine points or more, and needed one more to put the whole thing away. Round eight went 2-3. Then round nine turned into the game of the night: Renee C. and Whitney M. outlasted Candice and Angela H. 13-11, the only game all evening to run past eleven. Candice finished second at plus 32 and with the top night rating in the field at 76.

Niko took third on the same 7-2 record at plus 24, and she got there the hard way: after losing in round four she won her last five in a row, including a 3-2 grind over Candice and Whitney on court four in round eight. That game deserves its own plaque. Eight of the night's 36 games were decided by a single point, which is a completely absurd number and tells you exactly how evenly this field was stacked.

Tanya D. took fourth at 6-3 with a plus 20 and never dropped off the top court. Ivonne E. and Renee both landed 6-3 nights and both climbed two spots, Ivonne surviving a run of one-point games that would have broken most people. Whitney finished 5-4 with three comeback wins and a night rating of 69.

Further down the sheet there was plenty worth keeping. Angela went 4-5 but scored 65 points and finished a positive plus 7, with three MVP shares. Suzanne A. collected four MVP nods, the most of any woman in the field, across rounds one, two, six and seven. LS D. logged four comeback wins, more than anyone, and shared a plus 9 MVP in round eight after an 11-2 alongside Christyn. Cindy H. hung an 11-1 in round four.

Wendy I. climbed a spot and won a 10-9 thriller with Renee in round three. Larissa B. and Joanne M. closed the night together with an 11-6, and Joanne also picked up an MVP share in round six. Gia B. reached court four and stole an 8-7 in round five. And Tiffany S. finished 2-7 on record but at a differential of only minus 3, better than four players who won twice as often, with 60 points scored and MVP mentions in every single round.

For the season arc: this was the first women's-only ladder since the August Birthdays night on August 17, and it produced a podium so tight that record alone could not break it. Christyn banked 94 points, Candice 85, Niko 78. The differential tiebreaker has never earned its keep quite like this.
