Thursday Night Round Robin Recap

Jul 23, 2026 · South Bay Tennis & Pickleball Center · 8 players · 7 rounds · 2 courts

The Recap

Greg Shenkman Owns Court 2 Nearly Wire to Wire in a Two-Court Round Robin

Greg Shenkman went 6-1 and barely left the good side of the net, closing the night with a +23 point differential and a 4-match win streak to punctuate it. He held Court 2 for six of the seven rounds, with a single one-round dip back to Court 1 in Round 4 before returning. On a two-court Round Robin night with eight players, that kind of tenure on the top court is about as dominant as a run gets.

Kevin Vu quietly put up the best point differential of the night at +29, even though his 5-2 record left him a game behind Greg in the standings. Richard Hak and Matt P tied at 5-2 with matching +14 point differentials — the standings gave the higher spot to Richard Hak on the next tiebreaker, Dink Rating: 69 to Matt P's 68.3.

The loudest scoreline of the night came in Round 2, when Chandler Hong and Kevin Vu ran through Teddie Liu and Kayo Hayashi 11-1 on Court 1. Matt P spent five of seven rounds on Court 2 — tied with Richard Hak for the second-most tenure there — even though neither finished on top of it.

It was a rough one for Teddie Liu, who finished 1-6 with a -30 point differential, and Katie Frisinger and Kayo Hayashi weren't far behind at 2-5 apiece. All three spent most of the night on Court 1 and rarely got a turn on the other side.
