August Birthdays Women's Ladder Recap

Aug 17, 2026 · South End Racquet and Health Club · 20 players · 7 rounds · 5 courts

The Recap

Stacey L Climbs From Court 2 to a Perfect 7-0 on Birthday Night

Stacey L did not start the night on the kitchen court — she started on Court 2, three rungs down. Then she won everything. Seven matches, seven wins, the only unbeaten card on a night where nobody else finished better than 5-2. She climbed a court a round until she reached Court 5 in Round 4 and never came back down, closing with a +45 point differential and a Dink Rating of 88.2, both the best in the building by a wide margin.

“She started three rungs down. Then she won everything.”

Behind her, three players finished 5-2 and the standings had to separate them on point differential. Fiat T took second on +27, Jobeth Zapata third on +22, and Angela C. fourth on +12 — despite Angela carrying the second-highest Dink Rating of the night at 78.4. The three took completely different routes: Fiat opened on Court 5 and spent five of seven rounds there, Jobeth worked up from Court 3 and only reached the kitchen twice, and Angela slid from Court 4 to Court 3 by the finish.

The middle of the field was a genuine logjam — seven players tied at 4-3, sorted entirely on differential from Ivonne's +21 down to Candice's -3. Ivonne's night was the most improbable of them: she started on Court 1, the bottom of the ladder, climbed to Court 3, and posted the best differential of anyone outside the top four without ever once reaching the kitchen court.

Joanne Moore tied Fiat T for the most kitchen tenure of the night — five of seven rounds on Court 5 — and still finished sixth at 4-3, which is the ladder format working exactly as intended: holding the top court is hard, and holding it doesn't guarantee the win. Going the other direction, Larissa B had the night's steepest slide, opening on Court 5 and finishing on Court 2.

At the bottom, Shelby Morrisroe took the worst of it on record — 1-6, with four straight rounds parked on Court 1 — while Leanne Hoffman owned the night's worst differential at -34. The two heaviest blows both ended 11-0: Ivonne & Cindy Hsieh shut out Lisa Doyle & Leanne Hoffman on Court 1 in Round 1, and Jobeth Zapata & Ivonne did it again to Cindy Hsieh & Gia Boysen on Court 3 in Round 5 — Ivonne on the right side of both. And a note on how quickly a ladder turns over: Sharon, who won the division's debut night on July 28 at 6-1, went 2-5 here and fell from Court 3 to Court 1.
