Aug 27, 2026 · South Bay Tennis & Pickleball Center · 16 players · 8 fixed pairs · 10 rounds · 4 courts

The Recap

Fifth on Paper, Perfect on the Court: Ryan & Annie Own the First Fixed Partner Night

On the circuit’s first Fixed Partner night, Ryan Hom & Annie Kang played a perfect game of king of the hill. The random draw seeded them onto the top court — Court 3D, the kitchen — for round 1, and they simply never left: ten rounds, ten wins, all of them on the kitchen court. They were the only pair to crack 100 points (100–41, a +59 differential) and posted a night DR of 84.6, allowing just 4.1 points a game along the way. On combined DUPR they came in as only the fifth-strongest pairing of the eight (6.83) — nobody told them.

Phoebe Borsum & Kai Pylkkanen arrived as the strongest pair on paper (7.45 combined DUPR) and finished second at 6-4 — the hard way. They started on the bottom court (2B), blanked Antonette & Phil 11-0 in round 2 for the night’s only shutout, got knocked back down to the bottom court by round 5, then climbed all the way to the kitchen for round 10, where Ryan & Annie closed the night out 9-5. Their bottom-to-kitchen run was the biggest climb of the night, +3 courts.

Third place came down to arithmetic. Antonette Petitt & Phil Monahan and Eli Henry & Marissa Hardy both finished 5-5, and the tiebreaker — point differential — split them hard: +5 against −23. Eli & Marissa’s consolation was drama: they won two of the night’s four one-point games (11-10 in round 3, 7-6 in round 7, both over the paper-favorite pair of Phoebe & Kai) and made four separate trips up to the kitchen court — every one of them ending with Ryan & Annie sending them back down.

Down the ladder, Kayo Hayashi & Daniel Hui — the lowest-rated pairing on paper at 5.92 combined — ripped off three straight wins in rounds 2–4 and took the best of the three 4-6 records on point differential (−2). Shelby Morrisroe & Richard Hak made the second-biggest climb of the night, from the bottom court up to 3B, punctuated by an 11-2 win in round 8. Fiat T & Devin Carroll had the toughest night at 2-8 (−33) but banked both of their wins inside the final four rounds. The night ran ten rounds — two past the scheduled eight — and all eight pairs answered the bell for every one of them.
