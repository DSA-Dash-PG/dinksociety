Amazing Ladies Ladder #1 Recap

Jul 28, 2026 · South Bay Tennis & Pickleball Center · 20 players · 7 rounds · 5 courts

The Recap

Sharon Never Leaves Court 5 and Runs Away With the Ladies Ladder’s Debut Night

Sharon set the tone early and never let go. She went 6-1 with a +28 point differential — tied for the best of the night — and the highest Dink Rating of anyone in the field at 73.6. She opened on Court 5, the kitchen, and simply stayed there for six of the seven rounds, the only player who spent the entire night camped on the top court without a single trip down.

Behind her, three players tied at 5-2 and the standings had to sort them out on point differential. Tanya Deemer matched Sharon's night-best +28 to take second, Lisa Doyle's +23 was good for third, and Ivonne's +3 left her fourth — even though Ivonne tied Sharon for the most kitchen-court tenure of anyone on the night, six of seven rounds. Tanya's climb told the opposite story: she worked her way from Court 2 up to Court 4 and posted the second-best differential in the building without ever setting foot on Court 5.

Court movement was bunched at the top of the pack: Lisa Greene, Tanya Deemer, Stacey L, Tina O'Brien, and J'Mo all climbed two courts over the course of the night, the biggest jump anyone made. Tina O'Brien and J'Mo both started on the bottom court and worked up to Court 3 by the finish.

It went the other way for Sarah B, who finished 1-6 with a -29 point differential, the worst of the night, and slid from Court 4 down to Court 1. She wasn't alone on the way down — Suzanne and Tina Gross also fell three courts apiece. Court 2 was the night's launching pad for blowouts: Tiffany Snyder and Tanya Deemer opened the night with an 11-1 win there in Round 1, and the same court produced two more 11-1 results before the night was through.
