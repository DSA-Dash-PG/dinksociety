01 · Season registration

Full refund early. Half refund late. None once we start.

Team and free-agent registration fees for a league season follow the schedule below, measured against the season start date published on the registration page.

Cancel 10 or more days before the season starts
100% refund
Cancel between 10 days and 72 hours before
50% refund
Cancel less than 72 hours before
No refund
Once the season is underway
No refund
  • Deposits follow the same schedule as the full fee — a deposit paid on a team that cancels 10+ days out is refunded in full.
  • A team that hasn't paid its balance by the balance-due date may be held out of play; the deposit is not refunded once the 72-hour cutoff has passed.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method — the Stripe card or the Venmo account that paid — and typically post within 5–10 business days for cards, sooner for Venmo.
  • A free agent who can't be placed on a team is refunded in full regardless of timing.
02 · Ladder nights

Cancel from the reminder email and you get a credit.

  • Each ladder night states its cancellation policy when you sign up. The default is credit, not refund: cancel your spot using the link in your reminder email and a ladder credit for what you paid is added to your account for a future night. Your spot goes to the waitlist.
  • Some ladders are run as no refund, no credit; that's shown on the signup page before you pay.
  • No-shows are not credited.
  • Ladder credits have no cash value, don't expire while the ladder program is running, and aren't transferable.
03 · Cancelled by the League

Our cancellation, your money back.

  • If the League cancels a season before it starts, every registration is refunded in full.
  • If a ladder night is cancelled by the League (venue, weather, not enough players), everyone who paid gets a full refund or a credit — your choice.
  • Rescheduled matches or events — moved for weather, venue availability or unforeseen circumstances — are not refundable. We'll give as much notice as we can.
  • If a season is cut short after it's underway, the League will refund a pro-rated share of the fee for weeks not played, less venue costs already incurred.
04 · Removed for conduct

No refund.

A player or team removed from the League for violating the code of conduct, the venue's policies or the Terms of Service is not refunded any part of their fee.

05 · How to request a refund

One email.

Email dink@dinksociety.app from the address on your registration with your team name (or "free agent") and the season. Captains request on behalf of their team. The date we receive the email is the cancellation date for the schedule above. We'll confirm within 3 business days.

06 · Chargebacks

Please ask us first.

This policy is shown before every payment and agreed to at registration. If you have a problem with a charge, contact us before disputing it with your card issuer — we'll resolve legitimate issues faster than a dispute will. A chargeback filed against a fee that's outside this policy may result in removal from the League until it's settled.
