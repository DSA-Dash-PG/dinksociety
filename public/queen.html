Queen ladder rules
The same ladder rules as every Dink Society night — women's field only
👑
Women's field only
Registration is gender-locked. If your profile gender isn't set, signup is blocked with a note to contact the organizer.
🏓
Games to 11, win by 1
Every game is first to 11 — reach 11 before your opponents and you win, played within the round's allotted time.
⏱️
Beat the clock
If time runs out before either team reaches 11, the team ahead at the buzzer wins. Tied when time runs out? The team that reached that score first wins.
↗
Winners up, losers down
After each round, winning teams rise a court, losing teams drop one, and partners split — fresh teams every round.
🏆
Who wins the night
1. Most wins. 2. If tied, highest point differential. 3. Still tied, higher Dink Rating (DR).
⬡
Dink Rating (DR)
A 0–100 skill score from your results — the final tiebreaker, and cumulative across every format you play.
📊
Where the stats land
Results build the Queen board, separate from the Society (mixed) board. Your profile still shows the Overall sum across every format, plus a per-format breakdown.
