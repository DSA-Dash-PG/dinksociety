How signatures are recorded. Each player signs the exact version shown above by typing their full legal name in the Player Portal after confirming they have read it. The league records the signer's name, email, the waiver version, the season, and a timestamp with each signature and keeps an unalterable audit copy. Any edit to the waiver text creates a new version and requires every player to sign again. Signed paper copies are also accepted and are logged by league staff against the same record.
