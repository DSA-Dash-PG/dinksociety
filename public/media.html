01 · What we do

We photograph and film every event.

Part of what makes the League fun is the content: match photos, highlight video, the weekly recap, Player of the Week, the scrapbook. League staff, volunteers and partner photographers shoot at every league night, ladder night, playoff and social. Assume that if you're at a Dink Society event, you may be photographed or filmed — on court, on the sidelines, and in group shots.

02 · Consent

It's in the waiver you sign.

Every player signs the Player Waiver before playing. Its Photo & Media Release grants the League and its partners the right to photograph, film and record you during league activities, and to use your name, likeness, image and voice in any medium — including social media, the website, recaps, and promotional material — without compensation or further approval. Signing the waiver is a condition of playing.

Spectators and guests at league events are in a public setting and may appear in photos and video too. If you'd rather not, let a staff member know at the event and we'll do our best to keep you out of frame.

03 · Where it goes

The site, Instagram, and league communications.

  • dinksociety.app — galleries, match pages, recaps, player and team pages, the scrapbook.
  • Instagram @dinksociety.pb and other League social accounts, including stories and reels.
  • League emails: recaps, Player of the Week, season announcements.
  • Promotional material for future seasons, sponsors and venues.

We don't sell photos and we don't license them to third parties for unrelated advertising. Sponsor and venue use is limited to promoting the League and its events.

04 · Take-down requests

Ask and we'll pull it.

The release is broad because it has to be — we can't re-clear every post. But we're not out to embarrass anyone. If a specific photo or clip of you is unflattering, shows something private, or you just don't want it up, email dink@dinksociety.app (or DM the Instagram account) with a link or a description of the post. We'll take it down from our site and accounts within 7 days, no questions asked. We can't recall content other people have already shared or reposted, and we can't remove you from group and team photos that other players are in.

Players who would prefer not to be featured in promotional material — as opposed to ordinary game coverage — can tell us in writing before the season and we'll keep you out of it.

05 · Your own photos

Share them, tag us, be decent.

You're welcome to take and post your own photos and video at league events. Tag @dinksociety.pb and we may repost. Don't post anything that would embarrass or harass another player; that falls under the code of conduct.
