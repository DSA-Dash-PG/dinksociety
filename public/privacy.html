01 · What we collect

Only what it takes to run a league.

The Dink Society ("we", "us", "the League") runs a recreational pickleball league and ladder nights in Southern California. To do that we collect a small amount of personal information, all of it provided by you or your captain:

  • Identity & contact. Name, email address, phone number, and gender (used only to fill women's / men's / mixed lineup slots).
  • Play details. Team, division, DUPR ID or rating if you give us one, match results, game scores, standings, badges and season stats. Results and standings are public by design — that's the league.
  • Payments. Registration and ladder fees are handled by Stripe or Venmo. We see the amount, date and a confirmation reference. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Waiver signatures. When you sign the player waiver online we record the name you typed, your email, the waiver version, the season, the date and time, and your device's IP address and browser identifier so the signature can be verified.
  • Profile extras you choose to add. Profile photo, paddle, hometown, a short bio.
  • Photos & video. Taken at league events by the League or its partners. See Photos & video.
  • Usage. Basic, aggregate counts of which portal tabs get used, plus standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) kept by our hosting provider. We do not run advertising trackers or sell analytics data.

If your captain adds you to a roster, they provide your name, email and gender on your behalf. You'll get an email from us the first time so you know who has your details and can sign in to review them.

02 · How we use it

To run the season and keep you in the loop.

  • Registering you, placing you on a team or ladder, building schedules and lineups, and recording results.
  • Signing you in. We use email magic links — no passwords — so we need a working email address.
  • Sending league email: confirmations, lineup and availability reminders, score notices, recaps, and announcements from the League or your captain. You can unsubscribe from non-essential email at any time; sign-in links and payment receipts are transactional and still go out.
  • Publishing standings, leaderboards, player pages, recaps and photo galleries on the site and the League's social channels.
  • Processing payments, refunds and ladder credits, and keeping records we're required to keep.
  • Enforcing the league rules, the Terms of Service and the venue's policies, and handling disputes.
  • Protecting the League against fraud, abuse and legal claims — including keeping signed waivers.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

03 · Who we share it with

Service providers, teammates, and the public parts of the league.

  • Your teammates and captain. Captains see the name, email, phone and gender of everyone on their roster so they can set lineups and reach you on match night. Teammates see your name and lineup.
  • Opponents and the public. Your name, team, division, results, stats, badges, profile photo and any profile details you add appear on public league pages. Email and phone are never public.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, under their own privacy terms: Netlify (hosting, data storage, email sending infrastructure), Stripe (card payments), Venmo / PayPal (if you pay by Venmo), Resend (email delivery), Anthropic (drafting automated match recaps from public results), and DUPR if you link a DUPR ID.
  • Venues and sponsors. The host club may receive rosters for facility access and its own policies. Sponsors receive aggregate participation numbers, never your contact details.
  • Legal. When required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety of players and staff.
04 · Photos & video

We shoot every event and we post it.

Photos and video are taken at every league and ladder event and are published on dinksociety.app, Instagram and other social channels, in recaps, and in League promotional material. Participation is consent: every player grants this in the player waiver (Photo & Media Release), and it's spelled out in plain language in our Photo & Media Policy, including how to ask us to take a specific post down.

05 · Cookies & storage

One sign-in cookie. One theme setting. No trackers.

  • Session cookie. When you sign in through a magic link we set a cookie that keeps you signed in on that device. It carries a signed token, not your password (there is no password). Signing out clears it.
  • Local storage. Your light/dark theme choice and small conveniences like the last tab you opened are saved in your browser only. They never leave your device.
  • Payment pages. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page for fraud prevention.

We don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers, so there's no cookie banner to click through.

06 · Retention

Season records stay. Contact details don't have to.

  • Results, standings and stats are league history and are kept indefinitely. Once a player is archived, their name stays on past results but they no longer appear on active rosters.
  • Signed waivers are kept for as long as a claim could be brought — generally at least four years after the season in which they were signed — because that's the reason they exist.
  • Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Contact details (email, phone) can be removed on request once you're no longer on an active roster; see Your rights.
07 · Your rights

Ask and we'll handle it.

Whether or not a specific privacy law applies to you, you can email dink@dinksociety.app from the address on your account to:

  • See what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it — most of it you can fix yourself in the Player Portal.
  • Delete your contact details and profile. We'll keep the minimum needed for league history, signed waivers and payment records, and tell you what that is.
  • Unsubscribe from league email. Every non-essential email has an unsubscribe link, or just ask.
  • Take down a specific photo or post — see the Photo & Media Policy.

We'll respond within 30 days and we won't treat you any differently for asking. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front. California residents: this section is our notice under the California Online Privacy Protection Act and, to the extent it applies, the California Consumer Privacy Act.

08 · Age

Adults only.

League play is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and a parent or guardian who believes a minor has registered should contact us so we can remove the account.

09 · Security

Reasonable measures, honestly described.

Data is stored with our hosting provider in encrypted form, in transit and at rest. Sign-in is by single-use emailed links that expire, so there are no league passwords to leak. Admin access is limited to named League staff. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your information we'll notify you as California law requires.

10 · Changes

We'll date every revision.

When this policy changes we'll update the effective date at the top and, for anything material, email active players. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept it.
