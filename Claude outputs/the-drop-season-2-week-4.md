**Body**

Six things changed on October 9, and each one is stamped on the Rules page with the date. Here they are in one place.

**The regular season is a ten-week double round robin.** It was a five-week round robin plus Rivalry Week. Week Five on October 15 finishes the first trip around. From October 22 the five continuing teams play each other again.

**Everyone gets one bye.** Five teams means someone sits each Thursday: Big Dink Energy on October 22, Dink or Swim on October 29, Bonkerz on November 5, the Hitters on November 12 and Smash Society on November 19.

**Championship Night is Thursday, December 3.** It replaces Rivalry Week and the semifinals. The top four after Week 10 qualify and the standings do the seeding: first plays second for the title, third plays fourth for third place. Every game that night is first to 11, win by two, so at 10–10 play goes on.

**Playoff eligibility counts Weeks 1–10.** It is still two different weeks on court. It used to count Weeks 1–6.

**Rosters stay open through Week 8.** Players can be added until Week 8 has been played. Rosters used to lock after Week 2.

**Tiebreakers, in order:** match points, games won, head-to-head, point differential, points scored. Head-to-head applies only when exactly two teams are tied; three or more skip it and go to differential. The standings have used head-to-head all season, so no team's position changes.

The first rematches arrive on October 22: Dink or Swim against the Smash Society team that swept them in Week Three, and Bonkerz against the Hitters two weeks after Bonkerz won it 3–1. Dink or Swim and Bonkerz, who tied 2–2 in Week Two, meet again on the last night of the regular season.
