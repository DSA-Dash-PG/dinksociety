Queen ladder rules
Same ladder rules as every Dink Society night — women's field only
👑
Women's field only
Registration is gender-locked. If your profile gender isn't set, the signup is blocked with a note to contact the organizer.
🏓
Games to 11, win by 1
First to 11 wins, played within the round's allotted time.
⏱️
Beat the clock
Team ahead at the buzzer wins. Tied? Whoever reached that score first.
🏆
Who wins the night
1. Most wins. 2. Point differential. 3. Higher Dink Rating.
📊
Where the stats land
Results build the Queen board — separate from the Society (mixed) board. Your profile still shows the Overall sum across every format, plus a per-format breakdown.
